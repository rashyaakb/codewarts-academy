Before you enquire

A few things
parents ask.

Does my child need coding experience?

No coding experience is needed to ask about a trial. Tell us their age and interests, and we can recommend a starting point.

Which course should we choose?

Scratch Adventurers is aimed at ages 7–10, Minecraft Makers at 8–13 and Python Pioneers at 11–17. These are starting guides; ask about the best fit for your child.

What happens in the free trial?

The trial is a live, one-to-one 30-minute Zoom session: meet a tutor, try coding and discuss a suitable next step. The activity depends on the course and experience level.

Are lessons online or in a classroom?

Lessons are live and one-to-one on Zoom, with Codewarts based in Kuala Lumpur.

How much are regular lessons?

Fees depend on the course and experience level. Ask for the course fees and lesson options before deciding. Contact us for a confirmed quote.

What equipment or software is needed?

Ask us to confirm the device and software needed for your chosen course when arranging the trial, particularly for Minecraft Education. We will discuss setup before the session.

Does requesting a trial reserve a time?

No. An enquiry starts a conversation; your trial time is arranged and confirmed by message.

How can we prepare for an online trial?

Have a parent or guardian help arrange the session. A laptop or desktop with a keyboard is a useful starting point; tell us what device you have so we can confirm course compatibility. Test your internet and Zoom audio before the agreed time. We will confirm course software and account requirements, including Minecraft Education access, before the session.
